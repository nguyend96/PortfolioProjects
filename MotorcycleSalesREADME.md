# Motorcycle Parts Sales Dashboard | Power BI

This project analyzes motorcycle parts sales data using Power BI to deliver business insights across revenue performance, warehouse operations, product categories, and payment methods.

The objective was to transform raw transactional data into an interactive business intelligence dashboard that enables stakeholders to quickly identify revenue drivers, monitor sales performance, and make data driven decisions.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

This dashboard was designed to answer the following business questions:

- Which product categories generate the most revenue?
- Which warehouse contributes the highest sales?
- How do retail and wholesale customers compare?
- Which payment methods are preferred?
- How does revenue trend over time?
- What are the organization's key sales KPIs?

# 🔍 Key Findings

---

## Product Line Performance

### Top Performing Product Category
 Suspension & Traction

### Lowest Performing Product Category
 Miscellaneous

### Business Insight

Suspension & Traction generated the highest revenue among all product categories, making it the strongest revenue driver for the business. Miscellaneous products generated the least revenue and may benefit from further investigation into demand, pricing, or inventory allocation.

---

## Warehouse Performance

### Revenue Ranking

1. Central Warehouse
2. North Warehouse
3. West Warehouse

### Business Insight

Central Warehouse significantly outperformed all other locations and serves as the primary sales contributor for the organization.

### Central Warehouse Analysis

| Metric | Value |
|---------|--------:|
| Revenue | $141.98K |
| Share of Total Revenue | ~49% |

Nearly half of all company revenue originated from Central Warehouse.

---

## Customer Segment Analysis

### Revenue by Customer Type

| Customer Type | Revenue |
|---------------|---------:|
| Wholesale | ~$159K |
| Retail | ~$129K |

### Business Insight

Wholesale customers generated approximately **$30K** more revenue than retail customers, suggesting that wholesale accounts represent a critical revenue stream and strategic growth opportunity.

---

## Revenue Trend Analysis

### Monthly Revenue

| Month | Revenue |
|---------|---------:|
| June | ~$95K |
| July | ~$94K |
| August | ~$100K+ |

### Business Insight

Revenue remained relatively stable throughout the reporting period, with August delivering the strongest overall performance.

No major decline in sales activity was observed during the analyzed timeframe.

---

## Payment Method Analysis

### Revenue Ranking

1. Transfer
2. Credit Card
3. Cash

### Business Insight

Transfer payments generated the highest revenue, while cash transactions represented the lowest-performing payment method.

This trend suggests a strong customer preference for electronic payment options.

# 💡 Recommendations

### 1. Focus on High Performing Products

Continue prioritizing Suspension & Traction products, which consistently generate the highest revenue.

### 2. Replicate Central Warehouse Success

Investigate operational practices at Central Warehouse and apply successful strategies to North and West facilities.

### 3. Expand Wholesale Relationships

Wholesale customers represent a major source of revenue and should remain a focus for retention and growth initiatives.

### 4. Promote Digital Payments

Given the strong performance of transfer-based transactions, increasing digital payment adoption may improve customer convenience and operational efficiency.

#  Portfolio Summary

Developed an interactive Power BI dashboard analyzing motorcycle parts sales data across warehouses, product categories, and payment methods.

The project involved:

- Data validation and preparation
- KPI development
- Revenue trend analysis
- Warehouse performance evaluation
- Customer segmentation analysis
- Product category performance analysis
- Payment method analysis
- Interactive dashboard development

Key findings revealed that:

- Central Warehouse generated nearly half of total revenue.
- Suspension & Traction was the top-performing product category.
- Wholesale customers contributed significantly more revenue than retail customers.
- Transfer was the most profitable payment method.

This dashboard demonstrates the ability to transform raw business data into meaningful insights that support decision-making and operational strategy.
